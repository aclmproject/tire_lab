DISCUSSION
During the course of testing of the various tire configurations certain
phenomena were observed which are worthy of comment.
The "bow wave" and forward splash would not always dis._ppearwhen the
wheel "spun down." It appears that the test conditions _,,chas water film
thlckncss, load, etc., have an influence. The forward splash and bow wave
would disappear or be reduced under certain test conditions but not for other
test conditions.
The wheel would spin down to a complete stc_)under certain test ¢ondltions
(thick water films and a rough road surface) but generally slowed down to
some "Idle" speed. The Initial deceleration of the wheel on spin down Is
usually quite rapid and on the order of I000 feet per second for our 8-1nch
diameter wheel then decelerating at a reducea rate until its idle speed is
reached. Once the wheel is "idling" in the spun down condition the idle speed
