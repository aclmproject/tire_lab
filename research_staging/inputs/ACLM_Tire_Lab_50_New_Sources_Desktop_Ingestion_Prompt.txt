IMPORTANT OPERATING RULES FOR DESKTOP
1. Do not merely paste these summaries into the knowledge database. Open/read the source itself.
2. Deduplicate by DOI, title, URL, report number and bibliographic identity before creating any new source ID.
3. For paywalled/abstract-only sources, ingest only claims supported by the accessible abstract/metadata and mark FULL_TEXT_STATUS=ABSTRACT_ONLY. Never pretend full text was reviewed.
4. For open PDFs/pages, read the relevant sections in full. Record page/section/table references where practical.
5. Separate evidence into: measurement, observation, scalingRule, historicalConstraint, calibrationMethod, and source-level limitation.
6. Classify provenance distinctly: DIRECT_PERIOD_RACING, PERIOD_GENERAL_TIRE_TECHNICAL, MODERN_MOTORSPORT_SUPPLIER, GOVERNMENT/ACADEMIC_FOUNDATIONAL, MODERN_GENERAL_TIRE_TECHNICAL, or SECONDARY_HISTORICAL.
7. Do not convert a relationship into a numerical generator coefficient unless the source contains applicable numerical evidence and the conversion is justified.
8. Do not use passenger/truck/aircraft absolute values as racing tire absolutes. Use them primarily for mechanisms, scaling shapes, interactions and validation methods unless class applicability is established.
9. Do not silently raise source confidence because a paper is technical. Applicability matters as much as source quality.
10. Preserve observables: tread/surface temperature, bulk/core temperature, inner-liner/cavity temperature, pressure, and pyrometer readings are not interchangeable.
11. Research may strengthen the generator architecture without changing any historical family prior. "No numerical prior change" is an acceptable and often preferred result.
12. Persist approved evidence into the canonical Tire Knowledge master, increment the knowledge release, update SHA/hash and changelog, and ensure the shipped application actually uses it.
13. Do not bundle copyrighted/paywalled full texts into the public installer. Store citation/provenance and extracted evidence; keep any legally obtained development copies outside the end-user package.
14. At the end, produce a Research Ingestion Report with source-by-source FULL_TEXT_STATUS, extracted evidence, affected families/classes, proposed rules, rejected unsupported inferences, and a separate list of numerical generator changes (which may be empty).

RECOMMENDED PRIORITY
P0 immediate: pressure/thermal/wear mapping and current validation campaign.
P1 high: construction, load, transient force and wet/intermediate architecture.
P2 useful: broader history, regulations and calibration methodology.
