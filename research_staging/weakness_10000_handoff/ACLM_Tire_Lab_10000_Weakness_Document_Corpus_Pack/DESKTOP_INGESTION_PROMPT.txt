DOCUMENT RESOLUTION
For each record in DOCUMENT_ACQUISITION_MANIFEST.jsonl:
- search the named repository/domain plus the provided query;
- identify ONE unique real document that materially addresses the target;
- store canonical bibliographic identity and stable URL;
- if full text is public, read it;
- if paywalled, use only the abstract/metadata actually visible and mark ABSTRACT_ONLY or METADATA_ONLY;
- if a legitimate connected/library access path is available, use it;
- do not claim full-text review when only an abstract was available;
- if no qualifying document is found, mark NO_DOCUMENT_FOUND.

NO COUNT GAMING
A search page is not a document. A duplicate mirror is not another document. A patent family is normally one evidence source unless distinct family members add materially different disclosure. Do not invent documents to complete a shard.

IMMEDIATE QUESTIONS THIS CORPUS MUST HELP ANSWER
1. How should the Tire Lab predict setup cold pressure from the simulator's actual cold→hot pressure behavior without confusing PRESSURE_STATIC, PRESSURE_IDEAL and setup pressure?
2. What period racing evidence exists for tread/pyrometer, carcass/core and cavity temperatures, and how should those observables be distinguished?
3. What historically meaningful life definitions can be supported: observed stint, competitive life, service/abrasion life, or another measure?
4. How should real historical duty map into Assetto Corsa VIRTUALKM/WEAR_CURVE under USE_LOAD=1?
5. What are credible vertical/lateral/longitudinal stiffness and relaxation priors by construction/era/class?
6. What actual wet/intermediate temperature, pressure, water-depth and durability evidence exists?
7. Which supplier compound/construction codes can be decoded for Dunlop, Goodyear, Firestone, Michelin, Pirelli, Avon and others?
8. Can we build an event-specific 1992-99 GT/BPR/Le Mans/IMSA tire map instead of one generic 1990s GT tire?

SPECIAL REPOSITORIES TO MINE
- FIA Historic Database: homologations, Period Appendix J/C, official bulletins, period regulations, Formula One regulations and tire homologations.
- Motor Sport Magazine period archive: race reports and technical features with compound codes, pressure, pyrometer, supplier, wet/intermediate and failure observations.
- SAE Mobilus: manufacturer/government/academic tire mechanics, pressure, thermal, wear, wet and motorsports papers.
- Tire Science & Technology / Tire Society archive.
- NASA NTRS, NHTSA/NIST, EPA and ROSAP government technical corpora.
- Manufacturer archives/catalogs/technical bulletins/patents: Dunlop, Goodyear, Firestone/Bridgestone, Michelin, Pirelli, Avon.
- Google Patents for period construction/material disclosures, with strict fitment caveats.
- Calspan/FSAE TTC methodology as a calibration/test-method reference, not historical racing truth.
- Official Le Mans/FIA/IMSA/event records and period entry/program sources for supplier/stint/fitment evidence.
