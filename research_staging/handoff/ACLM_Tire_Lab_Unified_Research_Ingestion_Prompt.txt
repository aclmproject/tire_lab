3. EVIDENCE TYPES
============================================================

Promote evidence into explicit typed records rather than unstructured prose.

Support at least:

MEASUREMENT
Direct numerical measurement or table value.

OBSERVATION
Source-supported non-numeric or qualitative technical observation.

SCALING_RULE
Evidence-backed relationship or directional/functional behavior.

HISTORICAL_CONSTRAINT
A rule the historical generator should not violate: construction, dimensions, supplier/event identity, compound availability, regulations, warmers, etc.

FITMENT_EVIDENCE
Vehicle/event/tire dimensions and configuration evidence.

EVENT_SUPPLIER_EVIDENCE
Supplier, compound/specification, change, failure, stint, weather/event context.

CALIBRATION_METHOD
How a manufacturer/team/researcher measured or correlated tire behavior.

CALIBRATION_FIXTURE
Only for ACLM's own simulator telemetry/regression evidence, never to be confused with historical evidence.

SOURCE_LIMITATION
Known applicability, method, accessibility, context, or uncertainty restriction.

Source quality and applicability are separate dimensions.

A high-quality passenger/truck/aircraft study can provide excellent universal mechanism/equation evidence while being unsuitable as a direct absolute racing coefficient source.

A contemporary racing report can be excellent fitment/supplier/pressure/temperature evidence for a specific event while being unsuitable as a universal tire-mechanics equation.

SOURCE EVIDENCE
