2. Historical profile

Series/class options are grouped by the selected historical tire family. A proven curated or researched supplier is selected automatically; otherwise the manufacturer remains General / Unknown for manual selection.

Historically valid tire menu

Optional compound names

Leave a field blank to use the researched series name. Custom names change AC NAME/SHORT_NAME and the report; they do not change the underlying physics slot.
Auto category uses the researched year + racing class to choose a Tire Lab historical family and its period-valid tire menu. It does not impose a modern Soft/Medium/Hard ladder.
Import preserves direct car/tire geometry. Historical category selection changes tire architecture, dry tire type (treaded vs slick), construction when the evidence is unambiguous, compound availability/names, and class-life wear priors. Supplier-proprietary peak grip is not invented from the category.

3. Car / tire geometry and load

Thermal load duty is calculated independently for each axle from FZ0 per tread width.

4. Operating setup

Generated cold pressures are solved from absolute pressure (ideal-gas relation) at the selected reference temperature. Imported AC cold pressures are reference-only and are not copied into the new tire.

5. AC/CSP output rules

Legacy thermal is the default here. The generator will not silently enable CSP Thermal Model v2. This keeps the current Supra validation path intact.

Terminal tire failure

When enabled, Tire Lab removes the unrealistic very-low-grip tail after the final configured normal-wear point at or above the selected threshold, then adds one final point a short virtual-distance later. The default RAC-style behavior is a 0.20 vKm gap followed by 50% grip. This is a terminal casing/tread-failure simulation, not literal air-pressure loss.
Output ZIP name follows the car name. Every exported pack includes a car-named Historical Tire Accuracy & Evidence PDF report.

About & methodology

ACLM Historical Tire Lab is an independent, evidence-guided simulation and research project. It combines public historical sources, user-supplied Assetto Corsa physics and explicitly identified modeling assumptions.

Public evidence, not confidential data. Tire Lab does not use, claim access to or imply possession of confidential or proprietary manufacturer information.
  • Generated physics are historical reconstructions, not recovered manufacturer engineering models.
  • Manufacturer and series names are used for historical identification only; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.
  • Documented fitment and construction may be factual, while grip, thermal, pressure and wear values remain calibration priors unless independently measured.
  • Generated packs should be validated with simulation telemetry for the specific car, circuit and conditions.
