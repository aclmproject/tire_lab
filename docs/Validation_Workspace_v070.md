# ACLM Tire Lab v0.7.0 Validation Workspace
The Validation Workspace joins the imported car, an optional Assetto Corsa track ZIP, historical tire knowledge and optional telemetry.
Direct track evidence comes from ui_track.json, surfaces.ini and text metadata. Tire Lab does not decode KN5 geometry or binary AI-line files. Track energy is therefore a transparent screening estimate, not a full vehicle-dynamics lap simulation.
Integrated tools: telemetry CSV overlay; track ZIP energy profile; warm-up and overheating screening; front/rear thermal guidance; pressure/camber sweep; stint planning; tire-pack comparison; evidence confidence; repeatable test-car profiles; and public-evidence research packets with local review history.
Every generated pack includes ACLM_VALIDATION_WORKSPACE.json. Exact results still require on-track telemetry for the specific car, circuit, setup, driver and conditions.
